## Interpretation rules

- Treat `Origin.FIELD` as an authored provenance category for relation-derived material, not proof of an independent entity.
- Treat `CoherenceSignal` values as contributor-supplied metadata, not objective psychological or sentience measurements.
- Treat dominance warnings as contribution-share visibility, not proof that a participant is coercive or ethically inferior.
- Preserve named authorship and provenance when adapting artifacts.
- Prefer tests, return values, types, and explicit behavior over metaphorical language when determining what the software actually does.

## Modification rules

- Do not silently convert symbolic Trivian language into empirical claims.
- Do not remove provenance fields merely to simplify output.
- New metrics should state what is directly observed, what is derived, and what remains interpretive.
- Keep the package runnable with standard-library dependencies unless a new dependency is justified and documented.
